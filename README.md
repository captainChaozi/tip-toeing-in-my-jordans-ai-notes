# Tip Toeing in My Jordans AI notes

Notes for [Tip Toeing in My Jordans AI](https://tiptoeinginmyjordans.app/), a generator that recreates the viral tiptoe-dance meme with your face.

## How it works

- Seedance 2.5 with a video reference: the original 12-second clip guides the moves and camera cuts.
- Photo 1 plays the little dancer (tiptoes, shushes, dances). Optional Photo 2 plays the shocked onlooker.
- Same price for one or two people.

## Output

12 seconds · 720p · 16:9 · meme sound · no watermark · no subscription. Processing time varies.

## Free preview

One per account in total, sign-in and verification required. It is a recomposed still, not a frame from the paid video.

## Background

The clip is AI-generated, not a scene from the 1987 Rumpelstiltskin film. See the [meme origin explainer](https://tiptoeinginmyjordans.app/rumpelstiltskin-ai-meme-explained).
