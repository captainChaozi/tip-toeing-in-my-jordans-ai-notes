# Accounts, sign-in and payments

Reference for [Tip Toeing in My Jordans AI](https://tiptoeinginmyjordans.app/), summarised from its [privacy policy](https://tiptoeinginmyjordans.app/privacy-policy) and [meme explainer](https://tiptoeinginmyjordans.app/rumpelstiltskin-ai-meme-explained) (checked 2026-11-01).

## Sign-in

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Methods | Google sign-in, or email + password |
| Emails | Verification and password-reset emails via Cloudflare Email Service |
| Bot protection | Cloudflare Turnstile on registration and free-preview requests |
| Stored | Account name, email, sign-in details, order history |

## Packages (one-time, no subscription)

| Package | Price | Per video |
|---|---|---|
| 1 video | $9.90 | $9.90 |
| 3 videos | $19.90 | ≈ $6.63 |
| 10 videos | $44.90 | $4.49 |

- Solo (one photo) and duo (two photos) each use **1 video allowance**.
- Output: 12 s, 720p, 16:9 MP4 with meme audio, no watermark.
- Free preview: one per account in total (a recomposed still, not a video frame).

## Payment processing

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Processor | PayPal (may process card payments in its checkout) |
| What the site receives | Payment status and transaction information needed to issue purchased videos |
| Card numbers | Not collected or stored |

## Allowance returns

- Task marked failed → allowance returned.
- Completed but disappointing → not automatically returned; contact support first.
