## Version notes

- The AI short and the later song edit are different versions. The tool uses a short 12-second dance reference, not the whole story.
- The clip is not from the 1987 *Rumpelstiltskin* film, despite the costumes and aged-film look.
- The song is "Tip Toe Wing In My Jawwdinz" by RiFF RAFF.
- The latest prompt revision has not been publicly retested; published examples predate it.
