# Glossary: terms used on Tip Toeing in My Jordans AI

Short definitions of words you will see on [Tip Toeing in My Jordans AI](https://tiptoeinginmyjordans.app/), drawn from the home page, [examples](https://tiptoeinginmyjordans.app/examples) and [how-to guide](https://tiptoeinginmyjordans.app/how-to-make-tip-toeing-in-my-jordans-ai-video) (checked 2026-11-02).

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **The little man** | The tiptoe dancer role. Photo 1 always supplies this identity. |
| **The maiden** | The shocked onlooker in the straw. In *Me + a friend*, photo 2 supplies her identity; in *Just me*, the reference character stays. |
| **Just me / Me + a friend** | One-photo and two-photo modes. Both use 1 video allowance. |
| **Video allowance** | One paid video. Packages: 1 for $9.90, 3 for $19.90, 10 for $44.90. One-time payment; unused allowances never expire. |
| **Free preview** | One still image per account (shared by both modes), made with GPT Image 2. Not a frame from the paid video. Public preview files are kept 24 hours. |
| **Video reference** | The original 12-second clip that guides the moves and camera cuts, used with Seedance 2.5. |
| **Recomposed reference** | The comic barn still used for the preview, with your face edited in. |
| **Failed task** | A generation marked failed. Its video allowance is returned to your balance (not a cash refund). |
| **Share link** | A watch link anyone can open without an account. You can turn it off later; downloaded copies cannot be recalled. |
| **Gallery opt-in** | Public display in the site gallery requires your opt-in and approval. Ordinary share pages are not offered for indexing. |
| **Contact sheet** | A grid of frames sampled at four per second, used on the examples page to check drift. |

Output: 12 seconds, 720p, 16:9 landscape MP4, meme audio, no watermark. No commercial music licence is included.
